# MALSignature

### Your anime story. Made to share.

Turn your MyAnimeList profile into a signature that keeps up with you.
Your avatar, anime stats and latest shows—all in one little corner of the internet.

**[Create your signature →](https://malsignature.com/)** · [Leaderboard](https://malsignature.com/ranking/)

## Two sizes. Same you.

**Normal · 400 × 135**

![Zaczer_o’s normal MyAnimeList signature](assets/example-normal.png)

**Compact · 400 × 63**

![Zaczer_o’s compact MyAnimeList signature](assets/example-small.png)

Enter your username, choose a size and copy the image URL, BBCode or HTML.
Your signature refreshes automatically as it’s viewed, so your links stay the same.
